Mathematical Formulation: Gaussian Naive Bayes Theorem

The classifier applies Bayes' Theorem with the conditional independence assumption between features given the class:

P(Y = 1 | X) = [ P(Y = 1) × ∏ P(Xi | Y = 1) ] / [ P(Y = 1) ∏ P(Xi | Y = 1) + P(Y = 0) ∏ P(Xi | Y = 0) ]

For continuous features, each class-conditional likelihood density P(Xi | Y = c) is modeled as a normal (Gaussian) distribution:

P(Xi | Y = c) = (1 / √(2πσc,i2)) × exp( - (Xi - μc,i)2 / (2σc,i2) )
